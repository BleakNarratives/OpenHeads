SECTION 1 — HARD BLOCKERS (nothing monetizes until these are done)
================================================================================

These gate everything downstream. Everything else on this list is optional until
these are resolved. If you do nothing else this month, do these.

[ ] 1.1  GET A KYC-CAPABLE IDENTITY DOCUMENT
    Why it blocks: without it you cannot open a payment account, accept a
      payout, or receive money from any platform. Every monetization path in
      this account terminates here. This is the single highest-leverage item
      on this page.
    Exactly what to do:
      a. Check your state's DMV or ID office for a fee waiver, reduced-fee, or
         homeless/indigent ID program. Most states have one. Almost nobody
         knows. Search: "<your state> homeless ID fee waiver"
      b. If you need a birth certificate first: your state's vital records
         office, usually the cheapest starting point.
      c. Social Security card replacement is free and often online.
      d. Proof of address is the usual sticking point when homeless. Working
         options: a shelter or day-center letter, a nonprofit letter on
         letterhead, a signed affidavit (some states), or a PO box written in
         street-address format ("123 Main St #456").
    Time: varies, often same-day for the ID once documents are in hand
    Cost: $0-$40 depending on state; many waive it entirely
    Done when: you hold a physical government photo ID
    Note: an agent cannot do any part of this. It can look up your specific
      state's program, phone number, and hours if you tell it your state.

[ ] 1.2  OPEN AN ACCOUNT THAT CAN RECEIVE MONEY
    Why it blocks: even with an ID, you need a destination for funds.
    Exactly what to do:
      a. A no-fee prepaid account that accepts direct deposits is usually
         faster than a bank when you have no address history.
      b. Once ID is in hand, most fintech accounts open in minutes.
    Time: under an hour once 1.1 is done
    Cost: $0
    Done when: a test deposit lands and you can see it
    Note: an agent cannot complete identity verification on your behalf.

[ ] 1.3  DECIDE THE FIRST PRODUCT TO MONETIZE
    Why it blocks: every repo in this account is half-built. Picking one is
      the actual unblock. The agent cannot carry the consequences of this
      choice, so it cannot make it.
    Options and honest read:
      a. ConstraintOps / Tribunal — verification-as-a-service for AI coding.
         Sellable now. Priced tiers already written. Buyer is a developer.
      b. Mrs-Higgins pipeline — local SaaS routing on Android. Novel, but
         needs a paying customer type identified first.
      c. OutClaw — pro se legal AI. Real need, real audience, but longer path.
      d. Code City — gamified codebase visualizer. Most fun, least proven
         willingness-to-pay.
    Time: one evening of thinking
    Cost: $0
    Done when: you can say the sentence "I am selling X to Y for $Z"
    Note: an agent can build whichever you pick. It cannot pick.

SECTION 5 — CONVERSATIONS ONLY YOU CAN HAVE
================================================================================

[ ] 5.1  Ask the local no-kill shelter where the pet food bank is.
    You do not have to need the shelter to use the food bank. Most have one and
    most do not advertise it.
[ ] 5.2  Call 211 (or 211.org) for your area.
    They maintain current lists of warming centers, ID programs, and food
    assistance. One call, several problems.
[ ] 5.3  Ask about expedited SNAP processing.
    Expedited processing exists specifically for people with no income and no
    resources. You have to ask for it by name.
[ ] 5.4  Ask a local library about computer access and printing.
    ID applications need printing. Libraries do this free.
