WHAT THIS FILE IS
================================================================================

This is the list of things that CANNOT be done by an agent, a model, a script,
a cron job, a webhook, an automation, or a sub-agent. Everything here requires
a human body, a human identity, a human signature, a human account, a human
decision, or a human conversation.

Nothing in this file is busywork. If it could be delegated, it would have been.
Every item here is a hard blocker sitting between this project and revenue.

Read it top to bottom. Pick ONE item. Do it. Come back and mark it done.
Do not read the whole list and feel bad. That is not what it is for.

SECTION 2 — IDENTITY AND KYC (the payment wall)
================================================================================

You cannot receive money without identity verification. This is the single
highest-leverage thing on this page. An agent can build the entire product; it
cannot be you at a KYC checkpoint.

Items that typically apply. Delete what does not, add what does:

[ ] State ID or driver's license — check if your state offers fee waivers or
    homeless/indigent fee reduction. Many do, and almost nobody knows.
[ ] Social Security card replacement — free, online in many states
[ ] Birth certificate — vital records office, often the cheapest first step
[ ] Proof of address — this is the hard one when homeless. Options that work:
      - A shelter or day-center letter
      - A church or nonprofit letter on letterhead
      - Some states accept a signed affidavit
      - A PO box with a street-address format ("123 Main St #456")
[ ] Bank account or prepaid account that accepts deposits
